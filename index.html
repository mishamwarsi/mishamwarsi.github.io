My Story

Hello! My Name is Misham Warsi. I'm an SAP Consultant with over 6 years of hands-on experience implementing and supporting SAP solutions across Finance, Logistics, and HR modules.

I started this blog to document my own learning journey and to share practical, real-world knowledge that I couldn't find in official documentation. My goal is to make SAP accessible to everyone — from beginners to seasoned professionals.

When I'm not writing about SAP, I'm exploring the latest trends in cloud computing, DevOps, and enterprise technology.
